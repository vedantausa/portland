What data is available?

Supplier, brand, description, bottle size, case pack, prices, discount terms, alcohol content and source date come from the downloaded postings. Grape/style and vintage are inferred from descriptions and can be missing. Country, region, tasting notes, availability and national-brand status are not consistently supplied; use keywords or research for those.

Default ranking is a discovery-opportunity score built first around fit to your requested retail tier and margin, then deal savings, opening cash, case commitment, unknown-brand discovery value and whether the supplier is already on your order. It is not a wine-quality score; consumer/market research remains a separate step. Whole cases only. All posted tiers are considered; discounts are never stacked. Zero extra cost means margins exclude delivery and other landed charges. Source effective month and supplier availability require confirmation. Mixed-product qualification is not inferred.
